THE BIGGER PICTURE

Respiratory wellness is not a single-product story.

Healthy breathing and overall wellbeing involve many factors. A thoughtful routine can include nutritious food, regular activity, adequate sleep, avoiding smoking and seeking medical care when symptoms warrant it.

Lungora can be considered as a supplement within that wider framework, not as a replacement for professional care.
